What’s the difference between Lite and Bold?add

Can I use AURA with my phone and laptop?add

Yes. AURA headphones connect wirelessly to your everyday Bluetooth devices, so you can move between your phone, tablet, and computer.

Cloud, Midnight, Guava, and Sage are available across both editions. Go with the shade that feels most like you.
