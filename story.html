The helpful bits

Good to know.

How do I choose between AURA Lite and AURA Bold?add

Choose Lite for light everyday listening and a straightforward, comfortable fit. Choose Bold if you want adaptive noise cancelling, spatial audio, and longer battery life.

Can I connect AURA to more than one device?add

AURA connects to Bluetooth-enabled devices. Exact connection features can vary by model and device; check the product details for the edition you’re considering.

How do I keep my headphones in good shape?add

Keep your headphones dry, store them in a safe place between listening sessions, and gently wipe the cushions with a soft, dry cloth.
